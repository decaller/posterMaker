# Product: PosterMaker

## Vision
A high-craft, code-driven factory for generating professional technical posters, AI recipes, and cheatsheets. We treat documentation as an art form—precise, accessible, and visually stunning.

## Users
- **AI-Native Developers**: Who want to "code" their documentation using Typst and JSON.
- **SREs & Architects**: Who need clear, high-contrast visual guides for complex workflows.
- **Academic Tinkers**: Who appreciate the "Claude-style" editorial aesthetic for technical notes.

## Brand & Tone
- **Register**: Product (Design serves the information).
- **Voice**: Academic, considered, precise, and organic.
- **Tone**: "Botanical Library" meets "High-End IDE." It should feel like a rare, well-kept manuscript.
- **Anti-References**: 
  - No SaaS-cream gradients.
  - No neon "Cyberpunk" tech vibes.
  - No generic corporate blue/white.

## Strategic Principles
1. **Clarity over Decoration**: Every visual element (box, line, color) must serve the hierarchy of information.
2. **Vector Integrity**: Everything must be crisp at 1000% zoom. No raster assets.
3. **Socratic Discovery**: The system shouldn't just "render"; it should guide the creator to better content via the Agent Interaction Protocol.
4. **Offline-First**: All themes and assets reside locally in the `assets/` folder.
